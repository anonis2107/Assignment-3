FAQ

Payments //see if you use dl /dt or summary for list
What payment methods are accepted?
Card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, or EFT.
Will EFT delay my order?
No delay with proof of payment. Otherwise, ~1 business day.
Is my payment info secure?
Yes — processed securely via PayPal or bank.
What if my payment fails?
Your order is paused and we’ll contact you.
Uploading Designs
How to upload Designs?
On each product page there is a button to upload a file from your device.
What files should I use?
High-resolution or vector files.
What’s the max file size?
64MB (larger files via Dropbox or email).
Ordering
How to place an order?
Just select an item, submit all the details and add item to cart. Then all you have to do is check out to place your order!
How do I place a bulk order?
Contact us via email (sales@thestickerprinting.com) with your order details. We will get back to you as soon as possible.
Can I cancel my order?
Only before approval or production begins
Will I receive an tax invoice for my order?
Yes A PDF version of tax invoice is emailed to your order email address.
Order Processing Time
How long does my order take?
3–4 business days production + 1–2 days delivery.
Do you offer rush orders?
Yes, we prioritise urgent jobs where possible.
Reselling
Do you offer reseller discounts?
Yes — based on order volume (deposit required).
